      Questions we get asked

      What sizes do the rotary rack ovens come in?

      Five sizes: 64, 80, 120, 168 and 336 trays. Tell us your daily output and we will help you pick one.

      What fuel do the rotary ovens use?

      They are diesel fired, with an efficient diesel burner for rapid heating.

      Do you have ovens for smaller bakeries?

      Yes. Our gas deck ovens come with 1, 2 or 3 decks and hold from 2 to 9 trays of 400 x 600 mm.

      Which mixers need three-phase power?

      The spiral mixers FLM-40H to FLM-80H and the planetary mixers FLM-50F and FLM-60F are listed at 380V. The smaller models are listed at 220V. Ask us to confirm voltage for the model you want.

      Can I get a full catalogue?
